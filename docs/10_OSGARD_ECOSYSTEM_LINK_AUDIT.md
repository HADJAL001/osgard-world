## Independent reputation signals observed

- ScamAdviser currently exposes a report for [senjorio.com](https://www.scamadviser.com/check-website/senjorio.com) with a caution recommendation and automated trust warnings including low popularity / young domain indicators. Automated reputation scores can produce false positives, particularly for new niche services; this is a review trigger, not proof of wrongdoing.
- ScamAdviser currently exposes a report for [gold-glamour.online](https://www.scamadviser.com/check-website/gold-glamour.online) that calls the domain “Very Likely Unsafe”, reports a trust score of 0, and states that it could not analyse the site's content. This is an automated heuristic, not a legal or security finding, but it is a strong reason to pause paid promotion until the owner verifies domain control, page content, service terms and contact details.

No comparable authoritative primary content was returned for most other exact domains. Search results for similar names (for example, miosa.ai) are unrelated unless the hostname matches exactly.

## Required verification before paid advertising or high-trust referrals

1. Confirm DNS/domain control from the owner's registrar account and check the exact HTTPS URL, certificate, final redirects and browser warnings on desktop/mobile.
2. Confirm visible business identity/contact details, product purpose, support process, privacy notice and terms on each destination.
3. Test the main call-to-action and expected user flow. Verify that redirects do not lead to parked pages, unrelated brands, unexpected downloads or forms collecting more data than needed.
4. Review security headers, third-party scripts, cookie/consent behaviour and any payment collection before embedding or recommending the destination in a paid campaign.
5. Resolve the SENJORIO and GOLD GLAMOUR reputation warnings with ownership and service verification before spending on acquisition or sending users into payment/contact flows.
6. Retain UTM attribution, but never place personal details, email addresses, access tokens or account identifiers in query parameters.
7. Recheck links before each campaign; external sites can change independently of this repository.

The Games hub keeps these first-party links because they were explicitly supplied by the owner. Its UTM tags attribute outbound clicks only; they do not certify the destination or measure a conversion unless the destination implements compatible analytics.
